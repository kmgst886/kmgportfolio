Introduction

The LightKeeper's Chronicles began not as a planned series, but as a simple class assignment—a storyboard pitch created for a Spring 2024 course. What started as a short narrative exercise grew into my first published graphic novel, and eventually into an ongoing trilogy still in development.

This process book documents the creative evolution of the project: from early sketches and narrative experimentation, to self-publishing, audience reception, and the expansion into a multi-book series. It reflects both my growth as an artist and storyteller, and the personal themes that guide the series—loneliness, trust, and the quiet courage it takes to reconnect with others.

Origins: The Storyboarding Pitch

Core Ideas

  • A lonely lighthouse keeper
  • Something mysterious washing ashore
  • A creature who looks frightening but isn’t
  • A growing friendship
  • A threat to their island that forces unity

Initial Concept

The first spark came from an assignment requiring students to pitch a visual story in storyboard form. I wanted to explore a setting that felt isolated yet full of unseen life. The key emotional seed was the friendship between unlikely characters.

Early Visual Experiments

Sketches focused on atmosphere: fog studies, lighthouse silhouettes, creature thumbnails, and daily life gestures. These drawings emphasized mood over detail—quiet isolation, soft salt air, and the glowing warmth within the lighthouse.

Drafting the First Book

Developing the Lightkeeper

Defined by routine and solitude. His isolation was protective, making his growing bond with the creature emotionally meaningful.

Shaping the Creature

Designed to be mysterious yet gentle, with rounded features, soft eyes, and textures inspired by seals, rays, and bioluminescent fish.

Class Feedback

Peers responded strongly to the atmosphere, emotional vulnerability, and the cliffhanger ending, encouraging further development.

Self-Publishing Book 1

Challenges

  • Formatting requirements
  • Preparing print-ready files
  • Cover design and typography
  • Promoting the book independently

Reception

Readers connected deeply with the story. Curiosity about the creature’s backstory and the cliffhanger ending motivated me to continue the series.

Expanding to a Series

Committed to a trilogy for my senior exhibition, each book exploring a different emotional phase of the characters’ relationship.

Series Themes

  • Book 1: The Sea’s Unexpected Gift – Be careful who you trust, especially when lonely.
  • Book 2: Whispers Beneath the Waves – True friendship is proven by actions, not appearances.
  • Book 3: The Lightkeeper’s Last Light – Forgiveness isn’t forgetting; it’s choosing to see the good in those who’ve earned it.

Worldbuilding Across Books

Deepened the island’s history, the sea’s mysteries, townspeople’s fears, the creature’s origins, and the lightkeeper’s internal struggles.

Book 2: Whispers Beneath the Waves

Focused on refining narrative tension rather than visual design. Explored broken trust, complex emotional beats, and the lightkeeper’s questioning of whom to rely on.

Art-wise, emphasized page pacing, shadows, dynamic compositions, and panel transitions to convey emotional distance.

Book 3: The Lightkeeper’s Last Light (In Progress)

Focus on earned forgiveness, redemption, and the lightkeeper choosing trust again. Includes a final threat to the island and a stronger partnership.

Current development: thumbnailing emotional scenes, redesigning the creature for its final form, exploring the ruined town, and creating dramatic lighthouse imagery.

Artistic Development

  • Drawing: atmospheric depth, confident poses, environmental storytelling
  • Writing: nuanced dialogue, pacing, emotional arcs
  • Comics/Layout: improved flow, intentional silence, dramatic page turns
  • Production: formatting, cover design, independent publishing

Reflection

The series started as a school assignment but became a multi-book project with real readers and emotional impact. Stories grow when we let them breathe, characters change when we spend time with them, and unexpected projects can define us. The Lightkeeper Chronicles is ultimately about choosing connection—even when it’s frightening.

Looking Ahead

After Book 3 is finished, plans include compiling a deluxe edition, exploring prequels or side stories, and bringing the series to conventions and classrooms. To be continued…
