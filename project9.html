Overview

This project documents the creation of a physical arcade machine, from the initial concept to the fully built, playable game. The process blends interactive design, physical construction, and gameplay development.

Project Summary

The arcade machine, "Treasure Pleasure," is a retro-style game designed for immersive, hands-on engagement. The project included concept sketches, side panel design, hardware assembly, painting, wiring, game development, and exhibition setup.

Original Game Idea

The original concept centered on a treasure-hunting arcade game with simple mechanics, fun rewards, and vibrant visuals. Gameplay emphasized fast reflexes and player exploration.

Concept Sketches

Early sketches explored cabinet design, side panels, button layouts, and potential game visuals. These served as a visual roadmap for construction and painting.

Field Trips

Visits to arcade halls and gaming museums provided insight into player interaction, ergonomic design, and lighting effects that enhance arcade experience.

Project Proposal

The proposal outlined project goals, budget, materials, and timeline, emphasizing both construction and gameplay development.

Sketching Side Panels

Designing side panels involved iterative sketching and color tests to create visually striking neon artwork that would glow under exhibition lighting.

Beginning Construction

Started with cutting and assembling the wooden frame, ensuring structural stability and planning for future hardware and controls.

Starting to Assemble

Mounted side panels and began integrating internal supports. Early assembly focused on aligning panels, buttons, and screen placement.

Piecing It Together

Combined structural elements, hardware mounts, and preliminary wiring. Adjustments ensured proper fit and functional design for the interactive game.

Carpet Design

Designed and installed custom flooring beneath the arcade machine for aesthetic cohesion and enhanced exhibition presence.

Painting the Machine

Primed panels and applied neon paint layers. Techniques emphasized smooth gradients and sharp contrast to highlight the cabinet under lights.

Adding In Controls

Installed buttons, joysticks, and wiring, ensuring responsive gameplay and durable construction for repeated interaction.

Making the Game

Developed the interactive game mechanics, levels, scoring system, and visual effects. Prototyped in software to integrate seamlessly with the arcade machine controls.

Game Idea Pivot

Adjusted gameplay based on testing, refining levels and mechanics for better player engagement and enjoyment.

Final Game Design

The finalized game incorporated polished visuals, responsive controls, and cohesive mechanics, fully compatible with the physical arcade cabinet.

Final Machine

Completed assembly, painting, wiring, and installation of the game. The neon side panels, responsive controls, and smooth gameplay resulted in a fully interactive, visually striking arcade machine ready for players.

Exhibition Setup: Black Lights & Stool

Displayed under black lights with a custom stool for immersion. Lighting enhanced neon colors, highlighting the hand-painted panels and creating an engaging player experience.

Reflection

Through this project, learned hands-on skills in woodworking, electronics, painting, and game design. Combining physical construction with interactive gameplay provided deep insight into iterative creative processes.
