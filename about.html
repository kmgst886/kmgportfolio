About Me

Hi, I'm Kiya, a passionate UX/UI designer currently in my senior year, set to graduate later this year. With a strong foundation in design principles and a deep interest in how people interact with digital products, I aim to create thoughtful, intuitive experiences that not only look great but feel natural to use.

Throughout my academic journey, I’ve explored everything from user research and wireframing to high-fidelity prototyping and usability testing. My approach combines creativity with a user-centered mindset, and I’m always eager to learn, iterate, and grow.

Whether I’m sketching early ideas or refining pixel-perfect interfaces, I enjoy solving problems through design and collaborating with others to bring ideas to life. I’m excited to begin my professional journey and contribute to meaningful projects that make a real impact.
