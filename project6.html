Whispers Beneath The Waves (The Lightkeeper Chronicles) Paperback – November 5, 2025

Book 2 of 2: The Lightkeeper Chronicles

After the creature is locked away, the lighthouse keeper tries to move on—but strange things start happening again. When a charming new visitor arrives offering help, the town quickly embraces them. Only the lightkeeper is unsure. As more people begin to vanish, he must learn to trust his instincts—even when everyone else won’t.

Sometimes, a real friend tells the truth, even when it’s hard to hear. This story helps kids understand that true friends don’t always say what you want to hear. It also shows that trust should be based on actions over time, not just on appearances or charm.

The imprisoned creature may not have been the real enemy—and the new "friend" may be something much worse.
